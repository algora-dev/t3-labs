# T3 Labs Cookie Policy

**Last updated: 15 July 2026**

This Cookie Policy explains how **T3 Play Limited**, trading as **T3 Labs**, uses cookies and similar technologies on the T3 Labs website and private proposal pages.

## 1. Our default approach

T3 Labs uses a privacy-conscious proposal system.

Private proposal pages are designed to:

- avoid advertising cookies;
- avoid behavioural or cross-site tracking;
- use server-side proposal event logging where practical;
- self-host video where practical;
- avoid loading third-party booking tools automatically; and
- prevent optional third-party media technologies from loading until the visitor chooses to use them.

## 2. What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored on a device. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, SDKs and other tools that store or access information on a device.

Some technologies are necessary for a site to function. Others are optional and may require consent.

## 3. Cookies used by T3 Labs

### Strictly necessary preference cookie

T3 Labs may use the following first-party cookie:

| Name | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `t3_cookie_preferences` | T3 Labs | Stores the visitor's media or cookie preference so it does not need to be requested repeatedly | 6 months |

This cookie does not track browsing across other websites and is used only to remember the visitor's choice.

If the final implementation does not require a stored preference, this cookie should not be set.

### Security and delivery

Our hosting and security providers may process ordinary request information required to deliver and protect the website. This may include server logs, IP address, browser information and security signals.

We do not classify optional analytics as strictly necessary.

## 4. Proposal engagement without cookies

T3 Labs may record server-side events for a private proposal, including:

- page requested;
- video started;
- video watch milestones;
- concept section viewed;
- package reached;
- launch or booking action clicked; and
- proposal closed.

This event logging is linked to the proposal's Prospect ID and does not require a marketing cookie or cross-site identifier.

Ordinary web requests may include an IP address and browser information. We use this only for proportionate proposal measurement, security and reliability, and retain technical logs for a limited period.

A request generated by an email-security scanner is not treated as proof of genuine human interest.

## 5. Video

Where practical, proposal videos are hosted directly by T3 Labs. A self-hosted video does not require a third-party video cookie.

If a Loom or another third-party video player is used:

- the player is not loaded automatically;
- a poster image is displayed first;
- the visitor is told that choosing to load the player will connect to the provider;
- optional media technologies are not loaded before the required choice or consent; and
- the provider's own privacy and cookie terms apply after the player is loaded.

Because third-party providers may change cookie names and durations, the current media-provider details must be displayed in the page's Cookie Settings before the player loads.

## 6. Scheduling

T3 Labs normally uses a link to an external scheduling service such as Calendly rather than embedding the scheduling tool.

When a visitor follows the link, the scheduling provider's own cookie and privacy terms apply on its website.

If T3 Labs later embeds a scheduling tool, optional technologies must remain blocked until any required consent has been obtained.

## 7. Analytics

Private proposal pages do not use advertising or retargeting cookies.

T3 Labs' intended default is cookieless, server-side proposal measurement.

If T3 Labs later introduces browser-based analytics that stores or accesses information on a visitor's device:

- the technology will be added to this Policy;
- its purpose and duration will be explained;
- it will be blocked before consent where required; and
- visitors will be able to reject or withdraw consent.

## 8. Marketing cookies

T3 Labs does not use marketing, advertising or behavioural-retargeting cookies on private proposal pages.

No proposal visitor is added to an advertising audience merely because they opened a proposal.

## 9. Managing preferences

Where optional media or other optional technologies are available, the page must provide a clear choice before they load.

The available controls should include:

- **Allow optional media**
- **Continue without optional media**
- **Cookie Settings**

Rejecting optional technologies must be as easy as allowing them.

A persistent **Cookie Settings** link should remain available in the footer so a visitor can change their choice.

## 10. Browser controls

Most browsers allow users to delete or block cookies. Blocking strictly necessary technologies may affect site functions.

Browser settings do not replace the consent controls that T3 Labs is required to provide for optional technologies.

## 11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when our website, providers or technologies change.

The date at the top shows the current version.

## 12. Contact

Questions about cookies or proposal tracking can be sent to **insights@t3labs.co.uk** or raised by replying to a T3 Labs message.
