# Cookie Policy

**Last updated: 19 July 2026**

This policy explains how T3 Labs uses cookies and similar technologies on this website.

T3 Labs is a trading name of T3 Play Limited, NZ company number 9148617.

## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored on a device. Similar technologies may include local storage, pixels, scripts and tags that store or access information on a device.

## 2. Our current approach

The main T3 Labs website is designed to use as little tracking as reasonably possible.

We do not currently activate advertising or behavioural-tracking technologies by default.

External booking links, such as Calendly, take you to a third-party website. That provider applies its own cookie and privacy practices after you leave T3 Labs.

Where an external video or other third-party embed is used, it should remain blocked until the visitor chooses to load it.

## 3. Cookies currently used

| Name | Provider | Purpose | Duration | Category |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| `t3_cookie_preferences` | T3 Labs | Remembers the visitor's cookie or external-media choices | 6 months | Essential preference |

Do not add cookies to this table unless the website actually uses them.

## 4. Cookie categories

### Essential

Required for security, navigation, forms, requested functionality or remembering privacy choices. These cannot be switched off through the website where they are genuinely necessary.

### External media

Used only when a visitor chooses to load content supplied by a third party, such as a Loom video. The external provider may then set its own cookies or collect technical information.

### Analytics

Used to understand website use and improve performance. Do not activate analytics unless it is correctly configured, documented and, where required, allowed by the visitor.

### Marketing

Used for advertising, remarketing or cross-site tracking. These must remain off unless the visitor has made a valid choice allowing them.

Only show categories that are actually relevant to the live website.

## 5. Managing choices

Where optional technologies are used, visitors must be able to accept, reject or manage them.

A visitor can change their choice using the `Cookie settings` link where shown. They can also clear cookies through their browser.

Blocking some external content may prevent that content from displaying, but the core website should remain usable.

## 6. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy if we add or remove cookies, analytics, advertising tools or third-party embeds.

Questions about cookies can be sent to **insights@t3labs.co.uk**.
