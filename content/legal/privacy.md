# T3 Labs Privacy Notice

**Last updated: 15 July 2026**

This Privacy Notice explains how **T3 Play Limited**, a New Zealand company trading as **T3 Labs**, collects, uses, stores and shares personal information.

It covers the T3 Labs website, private proposal pages, business outreach, enquiry forms, meetings, website projects, hosting and support.

## 1. Who is responsible for your information

T3 Play Limited, trading as T3 Labs, is responsible for personal information collected for its own business purposes, including:

- prospect research and outreach;
- private website proposals;
- proposal engagement;
- T3 Labs contact forms;
- meeting bookings;
- orders, invoices and support; and
- operation and security of the T3 Labs website.

For personal information submitted through a customer's live website, that customer normally controls how the information is used. T3 Labs may process it on the customer's behalf as a service provider.

## 2. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

### Business and prospect information

- name;
- job title or role;
- company name;
- business email address;
- business telephone number;
- business location;
- company website;
- publicly available professional or social profiles;
- business services and public company information;
- the source from which contact information was obtained;
- communication history; and
- opt-out or suppression status.

### Proposal engagement information

For private proposal pages, we may record:

- the proposal identifier and company;
- date and time of access;
- pages or sections requested;
- video start and watch milestones;
- concept-gallery interactions;
- package-section views;
- launch, booking and removal-button clicks;
- basic browser, device and security information; and
- repeated requests to the same proposal URL.

We do not use this information to track people across unrelated websites.

A page request may be generated by an email-security scanner. We do not treat a page request alone as proof that a person viewed or accepted a proposal.

### Enquiries and meetings

- name;
- email address;
- telephone number;
- company;
- message;
- meeting booking information; and
- correspondence.

### Client and project information

- order and contract details;
- billing information and payment status;
- approved website content;
- logos, images and reviews;
- domain and account-access information;
- form-recipient details;
- approvals and revision requests; and
- support communications.

### Technical and security information

- IP address;
- browser and device information;
- server logs;
- consent or media preferences;
- security events; and
- spam and abuse signals.

We aim to collect only information reasonably needed for the stated purpose.

## 3. Where information comes from

We collect information:

- directly from you;
- from your company or colleagues;
- from public company websites;
- from public professional and social profiles;
- from public business and review directories;
- from reputable business-data providers;
- from referral partners;
- from website and proposal interactions; and
- from service providers used to operate T3 Labs.

Where we use publicly sourced business contact information, we aim to retain the source or enough information to explain where it came from.

New Zealand privacy principle 3A may require notification when personal information is collected indirectly. We generally provide access to this Privacy Notice in or with our first communication where appropriate, unless a lawful exception applies, including where the information is publicly available.

## 4. Why we use information

We use personal information to:

- identify businesses that may benefit from T3 Labs services;
- prepare relevant private website concepts and proposals;
- contact relevant business representatives;
- respond to enquiries;
- arrange calls;
- assess and confirm website orders;
- create, launch, host and support websites;
- issue invoices and maintain financial records;
- measure proposal engagement;
- prevent spam, abuse and security incidents;
- maintain records of objections and removals;
- improve T3 Labs services;
- comply with law; and
- establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

## 5. Legal grounds and privacy principles

Under New Zealand law, we collect and use personal information only for lawful purposes connected with our activities and only where the information is reasonably necessary.

Where UK data-protection law applies, we generally rely on:

- **legitimate interests** for proportionate business prospecting, proposal preparation, service improvement, security and suppression records;
- **steps before a contract** and **contract** for enquiries, orders and project delivery;
- **legal obligations** for accounting and compliance; and
- **consent** where optional cookies, media technologies or marketing law require it.

Our legitimate interests include finding relevant business customers, preparing tailored proposals, operating T3 Labs, protecting our systems and ensuring that people who object are not contacted again. We balance those interests against the rights and reasonable expectations of individuals.

## 6. Business outreach and publicly sourced emails

T3 Labs may contact business representatives using business contact information that has been publicly displayed or supplied through a reputable business-data source.

Where we rely on deemed consent under New Zealand's Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007, we do so only where we have reasonable grounds to believe:

- the exact electronic address was conspicuously published in a business or official capacity;
- the publication did not state that unsolicited commercial messages were unwanted; and
- the message is relevant to the person's business, role, functions or duties.

A database description such as “publicly sourced” is not automatically treated as proof. We aim to retain the available source and relevance record.

Every commercial outreach message should:

- identify T3 Play Limited trading as T3 Labs;
- provide accurate contact information;
- explain how to opt out; and
- use a functional unsubscribe method.

You may opt out by using the link provided or replying that you do not wish to be contacted. We will action the request promptly and maintain a minimal suppression record so that you are not accidentally added again.

For UK recipients, electronic-marketing rules differ between corporate bodies and individuals, sole traders and some partnerships. We assess the recipient type and applicable law before outreach.

## 7. Proposal tracking

Private proposal pages use a unique Prospect ID and company-specific URL.

We may record meaningful proposal events such as video play, watch milestones, viewing concept sections, reaching the package and clicking a call-to-action.

Our intended default is privacy-conscious server-side event logging without marketing cookies or cross-site tracking. We may receive an IP address and browser information as part of ordinary web requests, but we limit and retain technical logs only as needed for security, reliability and proportionate proposal measurement.

We do not use proposal engagement alone to make a solely automated decision that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

## 8. Cookies and media

Our Cookie Policy explains the technologies used on T3 Labs pages.

We do not use advertising or behavioural-marketing cookies on private proposal pages.

Where a third-party video player is used, it is blocked until the visitor chooses to load it and is informed that the provider may set media cookies. Wherever practical, proposal videos are self-hosted to avoid third-party tracking.

Booking links normally open the scheduling provider's own website rather than loading it automatically inside the proposal page.

## 10. International processing

T3 Play Limited is based in New Zealand and may use service providers in the United Kingdom, European Economic Area, United States or other countries.

Where personal information is disclosed outside New Zealand, we take reasonable steps to use a provider or arrangement that provides comparable safeguards or otherwise complies with New Zealand privacy principle 12.

Where UK data-protection law applies, we use an appropriate UK transfer mechanism where required.

## 11. Retention

We use the following standard retention periods unless a longer or shorter period is reasonably necessary:

- unsuccessful prospect working records: 12 months after the final communication;
- proposal engagement records: 12 months after the proposal closes or expires;
- active proposal page: normally up to 90 days unless extended;
- contact enquiries that do not become projects: 24 months;
- client contracts, invoices and core project records: seven years after the relationship ends;
- security logs: normally 30 days, unless needed to investigate an incident;
- consent or preference records: six months after the last update;
- customer enquiry data processed on behalf of a client: according to the client's instructions and the service arrangement; and
- suppression records: for as long as reasonably necessary to honour the objection.

We delete or anonymise information when it is no longer needed, except where it must be retained for legal, accounting, security or suppression purposes.

## 12. Security

We use reasonable organisational and technical measures appropriate to the information and risk, including:

- access controls;
- secure credential handling;
- encryption in transit;
- backups;
- logging and monitoring;
- limited access;
- supplier review;
- incident response; and
- secure deletion practices.

No online service can be guaranteed completely secure.

## 13. Your rights

Depending on the law that applies, you may have rights to:

- ask whether we hold personal information about you;
- request access;
- request correction;
- object to direct marketing;
- request deletion in relevant circumstances;
- request restriction;
- withdraw consent;
- request portability where applicable; and
- complain to a regulator.

Under New Zealand law, you have rights to access and request correction of your personal information.

Where UK data-protection law applies, additional UK GDPR rights may be available.

To exercise a right, email **insights@t3labs.co.uk** or reply to any message from us. We may need to verify your identity.

## 14. Complaints

Please contact T3 Labs first so that we can investigate.

You may also complain to the **New Zealand Office of the Privacy Commissioner**.

Where UK data-protection law applies, you may also have the right to complain to the **UK Information Commissioner's Office**.

## 15. Proposal removal and do-not-contact requests

Every private proposal provides a way to:

- close the proposal; and
- ask not to be contacted again.

Closing a proposal ends access to that proposal but does not automatically mean you object to all contact unless you select that option.

A do-not-contact request updates our suppression list and stops automated follow-up. We retain the minimum information needed to honour the request.
