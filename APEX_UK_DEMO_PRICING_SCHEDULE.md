## Engineering guardrails before exposing all-in totals

1. **Do not silently double-charge labour** when main covering labour already includes fixing accessories. Define explicit included scopes.
2. **Add new measurement groups** for pitched eaves, high edges, flat perimeter, flat abutments and outlet counts where needed. Existing supplier groups cannot represent all these.
3. **Avoid arbitrary low-pitch compatibility.** Each system needs manufacturer-specific approved pitch and substrate rules.
4. **Respect real product purchase units** in detailed quotes (packs, tiles, sheets, 6m kits), even if the quick tool uses normalised per-m² and per-metre rates.
5. **Exclude uncertain scope clearly:** insulation, rotten timbers, structural work, asbestos, leadwork, solar, chimneys, special access, gutters unless chosen.
6. **Separate new roofs from re-roofs** and apply removal by the *existing* covering.
7. **Price each lower and upper coherent geometry scenario independently**, then calculate a visible range; never apply a single generic complexity uplift to a detailed bill.
8. **Use fictional product names or clearly mark examples** if the demo price is not a verified quote for a branded SKU.
